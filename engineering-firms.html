§ For Engineering & EPCM Firms

Senior procurement support, without the hire.

Your engineers should be engineering, not chasing POs. I step into your project as a senior buyer on contract - cleaning up purchase orders and change orders, running subcontract administration, and making sure what goes to your client is right the first time.

§ 01 Core

Purchase Orders & Change Orders

I build, issue and track POs and change orders so they match the contract, the budget and the scope. Open POs get reconciled. Change orders get documented properly before the work happens, not after. When your client asks where a number came from, you have the answer.

§ 02 Core

Subcontract Administration

From pre-qualification to financial close-out. I flow down the prime agreement terms into each subcontract, check insurance, safety and WCB before anyone mobilizes, review invoices against contract rates and track commitments against the budget. I've administered subcontracts up to $5M across four concurrent EPCM projects, so I know where things slip.

§ 03 Clean-up

Procurement Clean-up & Close-out

Projects wrap up and the paperwork doesn't. Open POs, unsigned change orders, invoices that don't match, documents your client keeps sending back. I sort it out, reconcile every commitment and get the project to a clean financial close - so the relationship with your client stays strong for the next job.

§ 04 Team Support

Senior Oversight for Your Team

Growing firms often have engineers and junior buyers handling procurement on the side of their desk. That's how mistakes reach the client. I review the work before it goes out, set up simple processes that stick, and show your people why a clause or a PO detail matters. Your team gets stronger, and you don't have to add a permanent senior salary.

§ 05 Experience

EPCM and owner side. Both.

15+ years in Alberta oil & gas and major capital projects. I've worked procurement for an EPCM firm and on the owner's side in operations, so I know what your client expects to see and why.

Trans Mountain Pipeline · Inter Pipeline · Fortis · ExxonMobil · ATCO Gas Storage

Purchase orders · Change orders · Prime agreement flow-downs · Vendor pre-qualification · Insurance, safety & WCB compliance · Invoice review against contract rates · Accruals & cost tracking · SAP, D365, JD Edwards
