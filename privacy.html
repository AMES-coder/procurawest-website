Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Procura West Inc. ("I", "me") is a contract procurement practice operated by Amy-Marie Holland in Calgary, Alberta. This policy explains what personal information I collect through this website, how I use it, and your rights under Canadian privacy law.

What I Collect

I collect personal information only when you provide it directly:

  • Contact form: Your name, email address, company name, and message when you submit the contact form.
  • Email subscription: Your email address when you subscribe to receive updates, download a checklist, or complete a quiz.
  • Quiz responses: Your answers to procurement assessment quizzes, along with the email address you provide to receive your results.

How I Use It

  • To respond to your inquiry
  • To send you the results, checklist, or resource you requested
  • To send follow-up emails related to the topic you expressed interest in (you can unsubscribe at any time)

I do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

This website uses the following services that may process your data:

  • Formspree - processes contact form submissions. Their privacy policy.
  • MailerLite - manages email subscriptions and sends automated emails. Data is stored in the EU/US. Their privacy policy.
  • Gumroad - processes toolkit purchases. Their privacy policy.
  • Google Analytics - collects anonymous usage data (pages visited, time on site, general location). No personally identifiable information is collected. Their privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics - collects anonymous, privacy-focused usage data. No cookies are used. Their privacy policy.

Cookies

This website uses cookies set by Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies. No advertising cookies are used. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained as long as needed to respond to your inquiry. Email subscription data is retained until you unsubscribe. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link.

Your Rights

Under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information I hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Withdraw consent for future communications at any time
