Without A Process

Three things that happen when you buy AI without asking the right questions.

"We want to switch AI tools but we can't get our data out."

You get locked in

The auto-renewal kicked in. Your data can't be exported in a usable format. Switching to a competitor costs more than staying with a tool that doesn't work. You signed this contract 18 months ago and nobody read the exit clause.

"Wait - they're training their model on our proprietary data?"

Your data becomes their product

Buried in the terms of service: the vendor uses your company data to improve their AI. Your customer information. Your internal processes. Your competitive advantage. It's on page 14 of the agreement nobody opened.

"The demo was great. The invoice was not."

You overpay and can't prove why

The price tripled at renewal. You're paying for 50 seats but 8 people use it. The features you actually need are in a higher tier. When the CFO asks how you chose this vendor, your answer is "the demo looked good."
