Contractor Prequalification Checklist

25 items to verify before a contractor sets foot on your site. Built for Alberta construction and oil & gas.

Checked: 0 Yes  /  0 No  /  0 N/A

Legal & Registration

Valid business license on file
GST/HST registration current
Provincial registration up to date
Appropriate corporate insurance in place
No outstanding legal actions or liens

Safety & Compliance

WCB account in good standing
COR or SECOR certified
Written health & safety program
WHMIS training current for all workers
Incident rate at or below industry average
Safety orientation process documented

Financial & Insurance

Commercial general liability insurance ($2M+ recommended)
Vehicle and equipment insurance current
Professional liability insurance (if applicable)
Bonding capacity confirmed (if required)
Financial references available on request

Experience & Capability

Minimum 3 years of experience in their trade
Demonstrated experience on similar projects
Adequate workforce available for the scope
Equipment and tools sufficient for the work
At least 3 recent client references provided

Administrative

Signed master service agreement on file
Completed vendor registration form
Emergency contact information on file
Payment terms reviewed and agreed
Prompt payment obligations acknowledged
